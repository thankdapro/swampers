================================
SWAMPERS — UPLOAD INSTRUCTIONS
================================

You should see exactly 7 files in this folder:

  index.html         — main page
  styles.css         — styling
  script.js          — interactivity
  hero-swamp.jpeg    — hero background
  swamp-water.webp   — about section image
  swamp-foggy.jpeg   — news section background
  swamp-forest.webp  — forms section background

================================
UPLOAD TO GITHUB (overwrite everything)
================================

1. Go to your repo: https://github.com/thankdapro/swampers

2. Important: delete the OLD index.html and styles.css FIRST
   - Click on "index.html"
   - Click the trash can icon (top right)
   - Commit deletion
   - Repeat for "styles.css"

3. Upload the new files:
   - On the repo main page click "Add file" -> "Upload files"
   - Select ALL 7 files in this folder (Cmd+A in Finder)
   - Drag them into the GitHub upload area
   - Scroll down, click green "Commit changes" button

4. Wait ~30 seconds for GitHub Pages to redeploy
   (check the "github-pages" deployment in the right sidebar)

5. Open your live site and HARD REFRESH:
   - Mac: Cmd+Shift+R
   - Windows: Ctrl+Shift+R

================================
HOW TO VERIFY IT WORKED
================================

After upload, on the repo page:
  - index.html timestamp should say "now" or "0 minutes ago"
  - styles.css timestamp should say "now" or "0 minutes ago"

If timestamps still say "1 hour ago" or older, the upload didn't take.
Try again, making sure to confirm the "Replace" prompt and commit.
